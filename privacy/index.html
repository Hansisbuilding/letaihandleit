Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Let AI Handle It ("we") makes short comedy videos and publishes them to our own social media accounts using a small private posting tool (the "Tool"). This policy explains what the Tool does with information.

What the Tool accesses

The Tool connects only to accounts owned and operated by Let AI Handle It on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok, using the official APIs of those platforms. When we connect an account, the platform gives the Tool an access token that allows it to upload videos and captions to that account and to read basic profile information (such as the account name) and post performance statistics for our own posts.

What we collect about other people

None. The Tool is not offered to the public, has no user sign-ups, and does not collect, request, store, sell or share personal information about viewers, followers or any other users.

How tokens are stored

Access tokens for our own accounts are stored in encrypted secret storage on our private GitHub repository and on our own computer. They are used only to publish our videos and read our own post statistics, and they are never shared with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

We keep a record of what we have posted (post IDs, links, dates and performance numbers) for our own accounts only. Disconnecting an account in the platform settings, or deleting the stored token, immediately stops the Tool from accessing it.

Third-party platforms

Your use of Instagram, YouTube and TikTok is governed by their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy for YouTube.

Children

Our content is not directed at children under 13, and the Tool collects no information from anyone.
